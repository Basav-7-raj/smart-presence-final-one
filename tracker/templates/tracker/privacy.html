What is actually stored?

Registration creates a numerical face template. Recognition frames are processed in memory. Unknown-event snapshots are optional and are stored locally when Auto Capture Unknown is enabled.

This is a competition/demo implementation, not a legal compliance certification. Production deployment should add encryption at rest, retention policies, role-based access and formal privacy review.
